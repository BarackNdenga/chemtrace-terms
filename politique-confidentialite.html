Privacy Policy — ChemTrace

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This page reproduces ChemTrace’s existing privacy policy in its original language. The source policy is in English and was last updated on October 8, 2026.

i
Source document reproduced in English

The source policy’s statements are reproduced below. For questions about privacy, contact the email given in section 13.

01 Information You Provide

ChemTrace may process information that you voluntarily enter into the application, including information related to your experiments, such as:

  • Experiment names and descriptions
  • Experimental steps and procedures
  • Observations and notes
  • Measurements and results
  • Other information that you choose to record while documenting an experiment

You are responsible for ensuring that the information you enter into ChemTrace is appropriate for your intended use.

02 Personal Information

ChemTrace does not require users to provide personal information unless such information is necessary for a specific feature of the application.

We do not sell users' personal information.

03 Internet Connection

ChemTrace is an online application. Certain features require an Internet connection to operate.

ChemTrace does not advertise itself as an offline application, and functionality may be unavailable when an Internet connection is not available.

04 Camera and Device Permissions

If ChemTrace requests access to device features such as the camera, the permission is used to provide the corresponding functionality of the application.

Permissions are requested through the Android operating system and are subject to the user's authorization.

You can manage application permissions through your Android device settings.

05 Artificial Intelligence

The current version of ChemTrace does not use artificial intelligence or AI-powered analysis.

Any future AI functionality, if introduced, may require an update to this Privacy Policy.

06 Data Storage

Information generated or entered while using ChemTrace may be processed or stored as required for the application's functionality.

The exact storage behavior may depend on the features and technical architecture of the version of ChemTrace installed on the user's device.

07 Third-Party Services

ChemTrace may rely on third-party technical services necessary to provide certain application functions.

Such services may process limited technical or application data as necessary to provide their services.

Third-party providers are responsible for their own privacy practices and policies.

08 Data Sharing

ChemTrace does not sell or rent users' personal information.

Information may be processed by technical service providers when necessary to operate the application, or when disclosure is required by applicable law.

09 Data Security

Reasonable technical and organizational measures are used to help protect information processed by ChemTrace.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can guarantee absolute security.

10 Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the functionality for which it was collected or generated, subject to applicable technical and legal requirements.

The retention period may vary depending on the type of information and the functionality involved.

11 Children's Privacy

ChemTrace is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children.

12 Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when ChemTrace's functionality, technical architecture, or applicable legal requirements change.

The "Last updated" date at the beginning of this document will be updated whenever significant changes are made.

13 Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or about privacy practices related to ChemTrace, you can contact us at:

Email: ndengabarack@gmail.com
Application: ChemTrace

14 Summary

ChemTrace is designed to help users document and organize chemistry experiments.

The current version:

  • Does not use artificial intelligence.
  • Does not provide an offline mode.
  • Does not sell users' personal information.
  • Uses Android permissions only when required by the corresponding functionality.
  • May require an Internet connection for its online functionality.

By using ChemTrace, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy.
